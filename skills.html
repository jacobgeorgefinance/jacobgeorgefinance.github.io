NBA Fantasy Draft Simulator

I built a command-line draft for my AP Computer Science Principles class. Teams choose from a pool of 100 NBA players, and the program checks for duplicate positions while building a lineup. Once the teams are drafted, it simulates a matchup by varying each player’s score around their projected points.

About

I started this website to give myself a reason to research the questions I’m curious about and share what I learn along the way. As I work toward breaking into finance without family connections in the industry, I want people to see what I can bring through the work I put out.

I’m a first-year student at NYU Stern concentrating in Finance and Accounting. My mom is a nurse practitioner and my dad is a social worker, so I grew up seeing how their work helped people. Visiting my late grandfather’s retail store made me realize I could have that kind of impact through business. His decisions had real effects on the people and community who depended on the store.

At the Academy for Information Technology, a magnet school focused on business and technology, I studied global financial markets and learned to code in Python. As vice president of our FBLA investing club, my favorite part of preparing stock pitches was using the research skills I picked up in AP Capstone to learn about new businesses and industries. I also put my QuickBooks Online certification to work at my grandfather’s store by replacing its handwritten ledger with an online accounting system that made it easier to track sales and expenses.

As a multi-year varsity athlete in soccer and track, I liked competing against people who pushed me to get better. I’m drawn to the pace and intensity of banking, where I’d get to dig into how different businesses work and help owners make financial decisions that can change the course of a company.
